What is Dragon Ball?

Dragon Ball is a Japanese manga and anime series created by Akira Toriyama. It follows the adventures of Goku, a Saiyan warrior, as he trains in martial arts and explores the world in search of the seven mystical Dragon Balls, which can summon a dragon that grants wishes. The series is known for its intense battles, memorable characters, and epic storylines that have captivated audiences worldwide. Dragon Ball has become a cultural phenomenon, influencing countless other works in the anime and manga industry.
